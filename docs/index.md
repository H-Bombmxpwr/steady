# Steady

<p class="lede">A private weight-loss and fitness tracker for iPhone, built around an adaptive calorie budget and AI food logging. Everything you log stays on your phone — there is no account and no server.</p>

Most tracking apps ask you to weigh every gram and trust a textbook formula for how much you burn. Steady does the opposite: describe your dinner in a sentence and let AI itemize it, and let the app **learn your real burn rate** from what actually happens to your weight.

## What makes it different

**The budget adapts to you.** After a couple of weeks of logging, Steady compares what you actually ate against how your weight trend moved and works out what you're really burning — instead of trusting the equation it started with. See [Your calorie budget](calorie-budget).

**Food logging doesn't feel like data entry.** Type or dictate "chicken burrito bowl with guac," photograph the plate, or paste a recipe link, and every component comes back as its own line with a full nutrition panel. See [Food logging](food-logging).

**Training changes what you should eat.** A scheduled workout adds its burn back to that day's calories and its fluid needs to your water goal, and tells you carbs-per-hour for long sessions, what to eat before, and what to eat after. See [Workouts & fueling](workouts-and-fueling).

**It's genuinely private.** No account, no analytics, no server. Your log lives in a container on your device, progress photos sit behind Face ID, and the only things that ever leave are the meal descriptions you explicitly send for an AI estimate. See [Photos & your data](photos-and-data).

## The short feature list

- **Adaptive calorie budget** that learns your real TDEE from intake versus weight trend
- **Six ways to log food** — describe, photograph, paste a recipe link, scan a barcode, search a 3M-product database, or re-log a saved meal
- **Full nutrition panel** on every food, with calorie-density colors and a daily nutrition report
- **873-exercise database**, a workout builder with sets/reps/weight, and set-by-set logging with progressive-overload history
- **Fueling engine** for carbs, fluids, sodium, and recovery around training
- **Apple Health two-way sync**, including workouts from Garmin, Apple Watch, and Strava
- **Widgets, Siri shortcuts, and a Live Activity** for logging without opening the app
- **Face ID-protected progress photos** with an on-device timelapse builder
- **Streaks, patterns, and a Month in Review** poster
- **Five themes** with matching app icons, in light and dark

Requires **iOS 18 or later**. iPhone only.
