# Steady

**Private health logging for iPhone.**

Steady is a weight-loss and fitness tracker built around an adaptive calorie budget, high-protein targets, real workout programming, and AI food logging. Everything stays on your device — there is no account and no server.

### Common questions

**AI food logging isn't doing anything.**
It needs your own free Google Gemini API key. Open **Settings → AI Assist → How to get a free key** and follow the steps — it takes about a minute at [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com). Everything else in the app works without one.

**My steps or weigh-ins aren't showing up.**
Steady reads them from Apple Health. Check iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Steady** and make sure the categories you want are enabled. Devices like Garmin need to be syncing into Apple Health first.

**The widget is blank or out of date.**
Widgets refresh on a schedule set by iOS. Opening the app forces an update. If it stays blank, remove and re-add the widget.

**Where is my data?**
On your phone, in the app's private container. Deleting the app deletes all of it. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
