# Steady

<p class="lede">A private nutrition and fitness tracker for iPhone. Two apps in one: an adaptive calorie budget if you're losing weight, and training-driven fuelling if you're training for something. Everything you log stays on your phone — there is no account and no server.</p>

Most tracking apps ask you to weigh every gram and trust a textbook formula for how much you burn. Steady does the opposite: describe your dinner in a sentence and let AI itemize it, and let the app **learn your real burn rate** from what actually happens to your weight.

## What makes it different

**It knows why you're here.** Setup asks whether you're losing weight or training for something, and you get a genuinely different app either way — a budget and a trend line, or today's session with the fuel built around it. See [Modes](modes).

**The budget adapts to you.** After a couple of weeks of logging, Steady compares what you actually ate against how your weight trend moved and works out what you're really burning — instead of trusting the equation it started with. See [Your calorie budget](calorie-budget).

**Food logging doesn't feel like data entry.** Type or dictate "chicken burrito bowl with guac," photograph the plate, or paste a recipe link, and every component comes back as its own line with a full nutrition panel. See [Food logging](food-logging).

**Training changes what you should eat.** A scheduled workout adds its burn back to that day's calories and its fluid needs to your water goal, and tells you carbs-per-hour for long sessions, what to eat before, and what to eat after. Athlete mode goes further: it imports your plan from TrainingPeaks, periodizes carbs to each day's load, and sets hydration from **your own measured sweat rate** and the local heat index. See [Workouts & fueling](workouts-and-fueling).

**It's genuinely private.** No account, no analytics, no server. Your log lives in a container on your device, progress photos and your cycle log sit behind Face ID, and the only things that ever leave are the meal descriptions you explicitly send for an AI estimate. See [Photos & your data](photos-and-data).

## The short feature list

- **Two modes** — weight loss or athlete — with their own dashboards and their own target maths
- **Adaptive calorie budget** that learns your real TDEE from intake versus weight trend
- **Six ways to log food** — describe, photograph, paste a recipe link, scan a barcode, search a 3M-product database, or re-log a saved meal
- **Full nutrition panel** on every food, with calorie-density colors and a daily nutrition report
- **873-exercise database**, a workout builder with sets/reps/weight, and set-by-set logging with progressive-overload history
- **Fueling engine** for carbs, fluids, sodium, and recovery around training
- **TrainingPeaks import** — your planned sessions, with type and intensity read from the plan
- **Sweat testing and weather-aware hydration** from your own measured sweat rate and the local heat index
- **Carb periodization** to each day's training load
- **Cycle tracking**, on-device only and locked behind Face ID
- **Apple Health two-way sync**, including workouts from Garmin, Apple Watch, and Strava
- **Widgets, Siri shortcuts, and a Live Activity** for logging without opening the app
- **Face ID-protected progress photos** with an on-device timelapse builder
- **Streaks, patterns, and a Month in Review** poster
- **Five themes** with matching app icons, in light and dark

Requires **iOS 18 or later**. iPhone only.
