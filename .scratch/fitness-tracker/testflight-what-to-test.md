## Beta App Description (TestFlight → Test Information)

Steady is an all-in-one weight-loss and fitness tracker: an adaptive
calorie budget that learns your real burn rate, high-protein targets,
AI food logging (describe it, photograph it, or paste a recipe link),
workout fueling guidance, private progress photos behind Face ID, Apple
Health sync, and home- and lock-screen widgets.

## What to Test

Welcome to the first Steady build! I'm looking for anything confusing,
broken, or wrong-looking on your actual device. Poke at everything, but
here's where your eyes help most:

FIRST RUN
- Set up your plan (age, height, weight, goal, pace). Do the calorie and
  protein targets look sane for you?
- Settings → Appearance: try the accent themes and swap the app icon.

FOOD LOGGING (the heart of the app)
- Describe Your Meal: type or dictate, e.g. "two eggs, sourdough toast
  with butter, and an orange juice." Did it split into the right items?
- Photo of Food: snap a plate and check the items and portions.
- Recipe from a Link (new): paste a recipe website URL or a YouTube
  cooking video. Check the ingredients and the servings count, then log
  1 serving and try the servings stepper.
- Barcode scan and database search for packaged foods.
- Portion steppers: bump a portion up/down — every stat should follow.
- The green "Looked up" vs orange "Best guess" badge on AI estimates.

WORKOUTS + FUELING (new)
- Build a workout, add it to your weekly schedule with a type + intensity.
- Fuel Calculator (Workouts tab): pick type/intensity/duration and check
  the carbs-per-hour, before/after, and fluid numbers.
- On a day with a scheduled workout, look for the "Today's Fuel" card on
  the dashboard and the calories it adds to the budget.

DASHBOARD, WIDGETS, FEEL
- Swipe between tabs with the glass bar. Swipe the Milestones badges —
  they should scroll, NOT flip to the next tab.
- Add the home-screen and lock-screen widgets; tap the water button.
- The launch screen and the app-switcher preview should look themed
  (Steady branding), not a lock screen.

HEALTH, PHOTOS, REMINDERS
- Apple Health sync (Settings): weigh-ins, steps, and workouts flowing in.
- Progress photos with the Face ID lock — only the Photos section should
  ever ask to unlock.
- Notifications: morning weigh-in, hydration, workout, streak guard.

KNOWN LIMITS (don't report these)
- AI estimates are good, not perfect — every number stays editable after
  logging.
- TikTok/Instagram recipe links are hit-or-miss; recipe websites and
  YouTube are the most reliable.
- The training-day calorie bump shows on the dashboard but doesn't yet
  affect the streak check.

HOW TO REPORT
- Best: take a screenshot, then in TestFlight tap it and choose "Share
  Beta Feedback" — or just shake your phone while in the app.
- Or email me (address above). Please include your device and iOS version.

PRIVACY: only the food text/photos and recipe links you submit go out (to
Google, for the AI estimate). Your weight, progress photos, and everything
else stay on your device.
